# adebite promo: "five apps → one, with friends"

The brief for `AdebitePromo` / `AdebitePromoVertical`. The copy lives in `src/content.ts`
and the timing in `src/timeline.ts`.

## Positioning

- **Selling point:** a calorie tracker, a lift log and a progress tracker in one app, with
  your friends in it. You track the journey together and motivate each other.
- **Hook:** most people juggle a calorie app, a gym app, a run app, a scale app and a group
  chat. What if that was one app?
- **Offer:**
  - Refer 8 friends and Pro is yours for free.
  - The first 50 users get Pro forever. This is honoured with admin Pro grants, so the
    product doesn't change.
- **CTA:** adebite.com
- **Tone:** bright, playful and quick, in a short, friendly, plain voice.
